● Process

From brief to final cut.

You approve the important choices before I generate anything, so there are no surprises and no wasted rounds.

01

Brief and script

I read your brief, script and reference ad, then break it into scenes with timing, shots and on-screen text.

  • Scene-by-scene plan
  • English and Spanish versions
  • Claims and spelling checked
02

Cast and look

You pick the faces. I build character sheets and locations so every scene stays consistent.

  • Face options to choose from
  • Character and location sheets
  • Product drawn true to label
03

Generate and direct

I generate scene by scene, directing camera moves and acting in every prompt, and check each clip for errors.

  • Natural motion, no freezes
  • Lip-sync to dialogue
  • No morphs or warped products
04

Edit and deliver

I cut, add music and captions synced word by word, then export final files ready to upload.

  • Captions synced to speech
  • Hook variations for testing
  • Final 9:16 exports
